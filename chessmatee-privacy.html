Chessmatee Privacy Policy

October 12, 2026

Chessmatee is a chess app for iOS and Android. In short: your personal information never leaves your device. This page explains exactly what the app stores, where, and why.

Information we collect

Chessmatee does not require an account, sign-in, name, email address, or any personal information to use.

The app plays chess entirely on your device and stores only the following, locally on your phone:

  • Your current or most recent saved game (the moves played, which side you chose, and the difficulty level)
  • Your appearance preferences (board color, piece color, and app theme)

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos, and does not collect any device identifiers.

How information is used

The information listed above is used for one purpose only: to run the app on your own device — resuming your last game and remembering your preferences the next time you open the app. It is never used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app itself, and it is never shared with us or anyone else.

Data storage and security

All data stays in the app's local storage on your device — we do not operate a server that stores, receives, or could recover it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your device's system settings, permanently deletes it. Because this information never travels over the network, there is no transmission to secure.

Third-party services

Chessmatee does not use analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting, or any other third-party tracking service. The chess opponent runs entirely on your device and the app makes no network requests during normal play.

Children's privacy

Chessmatee does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app never asks any user — child or adult — for personal information, none is collected from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a revised date at the top of the page. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
