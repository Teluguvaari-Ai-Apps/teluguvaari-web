How it is calculated

Almost everything is worked out on your phone from the real positions of the Sun and Moon for your city. (Varjyam and Amrita Kalam apply the traditional per-nakshatra ghati tables to those positions.) We follow the conventions Telugu calendars use: the Lahiri ayanamsa, months that end on Amavasya (Amavasyant), and a Panchangam day that runs from sunrise to the next sunrise.

We test the calculations against published Panchangams and astronomical records, and correct anything that differs.

Traditions do differ in places — for example, which day to keep an Ekadashi. The app follows the Smarta convention printed in Telugu Panchangams. For weddings, house-warmings and other important muhurtham, please also confirm with your family priest.
