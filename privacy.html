Last updated: 18 September 2026

In short: Teluguvaari Calendar collects nothing about you and sends nothing off your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Every calculation happens on your device.

Who we are

Teluguvaari Calendar (“the app”) is published by Priyanka. You can reach us at desiaiapps@gmail.com.

What the app uses

Your location (optional)

Sunrise, Rahu Kalam, Varjyam and festival dates depend on where you are. You can pick a city from the list, or tap “Use my current location”. Only if you tap that button does the app ask your phone for its position.

  • It is read only while the app is open, never in the background.
  • It is used only to calculate the Panchangam for where you are.
  • It is rounded to about 1 km and saved on your phone only. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • If you allowed location and chose “current location”, the app refreshes the position quietly when it opens, so the times follow you if you travel.
  • If you decline the permission, the app works normally with the city you choose.

Your choices

Your language (Telugu or English) and your selected city are saved on your phone so the app remembers them.

What we do not do

  • We do not have a server that receives your data, and the app does not send data to any server.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or any device or advertising identifier.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not share or sell information — we hold none.
  • We do not need an account.

The app does not need the internet to work.

Stores and backups

  • The Apple App Store and Google Play collect their own information about downloads and crashes under their own privacy policies. They may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics; these contain nothing that identifies you.
  • If you back up your phone (iCloud or Google), the app’s saved settings may be included in that backup, under Apple’s or Google’s terms.

Deleting your data

Everything the app saves lives on your phone. Uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone’s settings, and it is gone. We hold nothing that we could delete for you.

Permissions

  • iPhone: “Location – While Using the App” (optional).
  • Android: approximate and precise location (optional).

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe/UK), the CCPA/CPRA (California) and India’s DPDP Act give people rights to access, correct and delete personal data. Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question, write to us.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information — for example by adding notifications or online features — we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches you.

మీ ప్రాంతం (ఐచ్ఛికం)

  • స్థానాన్ని యాప్ తెరిచి ఉన్నప్పుడు మాత్రమే చదువుతుంది — బ్యాక్‌గ్రౌండ్‌లో ఎప్పుడూ చదవదు.
  • మీరు ఉన్న ప్రాంతానికి పంచాంగం లెక్కించడానికి మాత్రమే వాడుతుంది.
  • దాన్ని సుమారు 1 కి.మీ. ఖచ్చితత్వానికి తగ్గించి మీ ఫోన్‌లో మాత్రమే భద్రపరుస్తుంది. మాకు గాని, మరెవరికి గాని పంపదు.
  • మీరు అనుమతి ఇచ్చి “ప్రస్తుత ప్రాంతం” ఎంచుకుంటే, యాప్ తెరిచినప్పుడు స్థానాన్ని నిశ్శబ్దంగా నవీకరిస్తుంది — ప్రయాణం చేస్తే సమయాలు మీతో పాటు మారుతాయి.
  • అనుమతి ఇవ్వకపోయినా, మీరు ఎంచుకున్న నగరంతో యాప్ సాధారణంగా పని చేస్తుంది.
